About FreshBite

Food ordering made simple.

FreshBite is a frontend-only food delivery e-commerce demonstration built with HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript.

🎯 Objective

Create a responsive real-world food ordering interface with browsing, cart and checkout interactions.

💻 Technology

HTML5 semantic structure, CSS3 responsive styling and JavaScript ES6 for interactivity.

🛒 Ordering

Cart quantity adjustment, totals, simulated checkout and order ID generation.

📱 Responsive

Designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

🍴 FreshBite

Fresh food, simple ordering and a smooth delivery-style experience for Hamirpur.
